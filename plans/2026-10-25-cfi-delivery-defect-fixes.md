## Summary

**Problem.** Two open bench-side defect groups, both about a mid-function entry delivering the wrong thing. (1) The landing-block family: a case's delivery block is entered dynamically (the jump/faked return is the attack), but the refactor left one block depending on a register an earlier guard proves — on RISC-V the guard and the delivery share `a0`, so the compiler folds the setup away and the status becomes the label address's low byte (214/50); a sibling's calibrated write now lands on the canary slot (262); and the CHERI lane's declared `1` on a third case is delivered vacuously (the lane records no frame-size, so the case never attacks). A mechanism-derived scan of the 16-source label family found exactly the one genuine delivery defect — the class is bounded and enumerable. (2) F2/267 is re-opened: the riscv64 path of the heap-memcpy jump case still faults at its own attack jump.
